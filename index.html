In the Mind of Marco

Welcome!

Welcome to my website! This is a place where I share my projects and ideas, along with some of my hobbies. I want this to be a place where I can document my journey, share my knowledge, and connect with like-minded individuals.

About Me

Hi! I am Marco Aguilera, a passionate developer and hobbyist. I enjoy creating web applications, photography, traveling, and learning new skills.
I am a senior student studying Computer Information Technology at California State University, Northridge. I am passionate about developing innovative solutions and continuously improving my skills in the field. I originally enrolled in Computer Science, but later switched to Computer Information Technology to better align with my career goals. In high school, I took coding courses that sparked my interest in technology and programming. I wanted to go into video game development, but I never had the opportunity to fully explore it. Instead, I focused on web development and other areas of software engineering that allowed me to apply my skills and creativity effectively.

My Life

I was born and raised in Los Angeles, California. My parents migrated from Mexico before I was born, seeking better opportunities and a brighter future for our family. I am the second of three children. I have an older brother and a younger sister, and we have always been close and shared many experiences growing up.

Growing up in Los Angeles, I was exposed to a diverse and vibrant culture that shaped my perspective and interests. I developed a love for community and social interactions, which influenced my personal and professional growth.

Childhood

During my childhood, I spent a lot of time playing video games with my cousins, playing outdoors, and engaging in various creative activities. Every Sunday morning my parents would take me and my siblings to church. I eventually grew to appreciate the sense of community and the moral lessons it provided.

Teenage Years

In high school, I made connections with people who I still keep in touch with today. I mainly met them through the cross-country team. I also participated in track and field during the spring season. These experiences helped me develop discipline and form lasting friendships.

In my senior year, I got my first job at El Pollo Loco. This was one of the best and worst experiences I have had. I struggled to balance work with my academic and personal commitments, which taught me to make significant changes in my life.

Future Goals

I hope to continue developing my skills, find a fulfilling job, and build a successful career that allows me to grow personally and professionally. I also hope to move out of my parents' house and build an independent life with my girlfriend.
